About

Brilliant Computer Education

Established in the Year 1995, the Brilliant Computer Education, an ISO 9001:2015 certified institute and is the only organization in and around Ongole, Prakasam District, Andhra Pradesh offers various career development courses and job oriented diplomas and targeting a well trained professional with the skills which meets growing computer industry/real time environment. Having vast experience in the field of computer training Brilliant is running from Past 31 years. It is one of the computer educational institutions with enduring Brand identity

Our vision is put into action through programs and a focus on environmental activities to benefit society, and a commitment to build value by making Brilliant Computer Education a truly sustainable company.

Our mission is to provide quality English language instruction through a variety of courses. Brilliant Computer is the optimal choice for your needs, offering nearly three decades of industry experience coupled with faculty members boasting over 20 years of expertise, ensuring students receive instruction from seasoned professionals. Their state-of-the-art computer labs are equipped with the latest technology, facilitating hands-on learning and practical skill development. Additionally, the institution provides a content-rich curriculum that is continuously updated to reflect the latest advancements in the field, ensuring students receive relevant, comprehensive, and industry-aligned education.
